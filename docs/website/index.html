Is it actually free?

Yes. Ghost Mode is free and open source under the MIT license. There’s no trial period, paid plan, or account requirement.

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone?

No. Ghost Mode communicates through Apple’s developer debug channel. You’ll need Developer Mode enabled and a trusted USB connection to set your location.

Which apps will see the new location?

Apps that read your iPhone’s simulated system location may use the new coordinates. Results can vary by app and iOS version; apps may also use other signals. Compatibility and undetectability aren’t guaranteed.

Does my phone need to stay plugged in?

You need USB to set the location and keep Travel Mode updating. The project documentation says a fixed location can persist after unplugging, until you restart your iPhone or toggle Location Services.

How do I get my real location back?

Restart your iPhone, or turn Location Services off and back on in Settings. Reopen the app you’re checking so it can refresh its location.

Which download should I choose?

Choose Windows for a .exe file. On Mac, choose Apple Silicon for an M-series chip or Intel for an Intel processor. Mac downloads use .dmg files. You can check your chip in Apple menu → About This Mac. Linux packages are not currently offered.

Which Mac do I have?

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. “Chip” followed by Apple M1, M2, or another M-series name means Apple Silicon. “Processor” followed by Intel means Intel Mac.
